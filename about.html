About the club

Casual play, strategy sessions, tournaments, and access to the firms that sponsor us.

Who we are

The CMU Poker Club is a community for students who enjoy poker, open to every skill level. Many of our members joined having never played a hand; others have competed for years. The club is low commitment by design, with no attendance requirement and optional participation in our general body meetings.

What we do

General body meetings are our regular sessions: casual play in a relaxed setting, usually with food. Alongside them we run strategy sessions on topics such as pot odds, position and bankroll management, and we host tournaments through the semester.

Connecting with our sponsors

The club is supported by GTS, Jump Trading, Jane Street, Hudson River Trading and Susquehanna. Several of these firms use poker in how they train and recruit, and our sponsored events give members direct access to the people who work there — a route to internships and full-time roles for those who want it.

The short version

  • Open to everyone Any skill level, no experience required
  • Low commitment No attendance requirement
  • Regular sessions Casual play, strategy and tournaments
  • Sponsor access Events with our partner trading firms

Our sponsors

The club is supported by firms whose work runs on the same instincts poker rewards — reading incomplete information and deciding under uncertainty.

Platinum tier

GTS

GTS is a technology-driven electronic market maker, providing liquidity across equities, ETFs, currencies, commodities and fixed income. The firm builds its own quantitative models and trading infrastructure to price thousands of instruments worldwide.

Visit GTS

Jump Trading

Jump Trading is a global algorithmic trading firm combining quantitative research, cutting-edge technology and an entrepreneurial culture. Founded in 1999, Jump has offices across the Americas, Europe and Asia, and has built one of the largest private supercomputers in the world to test its ideas.

Visit Jump Trading

Jane Street

Jane Street is a quantitative trading firm with offices worldwide. They hire smart, humble people who like to solve problems, build systems and test theories — and they're known for teaching new hires to think about markets through games and probability puzzles.

Visit Jane Street

Hudson River Trading

Hudson River Trading is a multi-asset class quantitative trading firm that provides liquidity on global markets and directly to clients. HRT has built one of the world's most advanced computing environments for research, modeling and risk management.

Visit Hudson River Trading

SIG

Susquehanna International Group is a global quantitative trading firm built around decision-making under uncertainty. Poker is part of how SIG trains its traders — the firm uses the game to teach probabilistic thinking and disciplined risk-taking.

Visit SIG
